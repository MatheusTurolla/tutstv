Tuts Clips – Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 25, 2026

Tuts Clips is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Tuts TV channel (@TutsTVOficial) to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

1. Data we access. When the owner connects TikTok, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (display name, username and avatar) through TikTok Login Kit, only to show which account is connected and to publish videos the owner chooses.
2. Storage. Tokens and keys are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to any third party.
3. Use. Data is used only to publish videos and show posting status. No analytics, advertising or tracking.
4. Other users. The tool does not collect data from any other person, viewer or TikTok user.
5. Deletion. The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time, which deletes the stored token. Access can also be revoked in the TikTok app settings.
6. Contact. https://www.youtube.com/@TutsTVOficial
